# 0008: Launch offer, name and providers

**Date:** 6 November 2026
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The owner closed four open points on this date.

## Decision

- D-3: the first 100 sellers get 50% off their first year. Renewals pay the full fee.
- D-4: Cashfree checks PAN, GSTIN and bank details, subject to confirming current pricing and coverage.
- D-10: the business name is Bid Market, in two words, subject to a trademark search.
- A-1: Razorpay, Cashfree, MSG91 and Resend are approved.

## Consequences

- The discount reduces the fee before GST.
- A launch discount of 100% makes the first year free, with no payment or invoice.
- The legal entity under D-10 stays open.
